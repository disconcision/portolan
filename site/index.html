Portolan

A canvas for reading OCaml code bases at a level above the text, and for working in them alongside agents. It starts from the Constellation canvas in Hazel.

A portolan is a sailor's chart: coasts drawn in detail from the routes actually sailed, interiors left blank. This page collects the prototypes, the notes that are kept current, and the one-off studies.
